About

Where creative taste meets production engineering.

I'm an AI creative producer and senior video editor working across direct-to-consumer brands — the kind of work where creative has to be sharp, fast, and built to convert.

My lane is the full production stack: I take a client brief and carry it through prompt architecture, AI generation, editing, and final delivery — from scroll-stopping UGC to long-form VSLs. I use Claude with MCP to my advantage, connecting it directly to tools like Higgsfield so generation, iteration, and delivery all live in one connected workflow instead of a dozen disconnected tabs.

Outside client work, I explore design systems for their own sake — coded poster series, landing page experiments, and whatever aesthetic is currently living rent-free in my head.
